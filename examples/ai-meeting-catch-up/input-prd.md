# PRD: AI Meeting Catch-up

## Overview

Turn a meeting transcript into a short summary covering decisions, action items, and unresolved questions. Users may ask follow-up questions about the meeting.

## Problem

Meeting transcripts are long. Existing AI summaries are often vague, omit decisions, and present suggestions as facts.

## Users

- People who missed the meeting
- Attendees trying to recall decisions
- Managers tracking actions

## User story

As a user, I want a reliable meeting summary so I can catch up without watching the recording or reading the full transcript.

## Goals

- Reduce catch-up time
- Make decisions and actions easy to find
- Let users verify every claim against the transcript

## Non-goals

- Producing formal minutes
- Automatically sending or assigning actions
- Supporting meetings without a transcript
- Making decisions on behalf of users

## Draft acceptance criteria

The summary should be accurate, concise, and helpful. Decisions and action items should be clear.
